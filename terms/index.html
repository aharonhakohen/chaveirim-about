SMS & Communication Terms

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Program Description

Chaveirim of Central Jersey (operated by Chaveirim Volunteer Services Inc.) sends operational text messages to callers who request roadside assistance (such as call confirmation and responder arrival status updates) as well as to volunteers, applicants, and members who opt in (such as dispatch notifications, scheduling updates, and account verification codes).

Opt-In & Consent

Volunteers and members opt in via our volunteer application or member authentication portals. Callers requesting live dispatch assistance receive transactional status updates strictly related to their active roadside call. Consent to receive SMS is never a condition of receiving emergency assistance or making a donation.

Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies based on call activity or account sign-ins. Standard message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.

Opt-Out & Support

Reply STOP to any message at any time to cancel SMS notifications; you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply HELP for assistance, or call (732) 370-2229.

Carrier Disclaimer & Privacy

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Please review our Privacy Policy. No mobile information or SMS opt-in consent data will ever be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
