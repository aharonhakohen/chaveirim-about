Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Chaveirim of Central Jersey, operated by Chaveirim Volunteer Services Inc. (“we”, “us”, “our”), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our websites, dispatch hotline, and operational call and text services, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

When you request roadside or emergency assistance, make a donation, or apply or serve as a volunteer, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, town, incident location, vehicle details, and call information you provide. Our websites and applications may also log standard technical metadata (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How We Use Information

  • To dispatch trained volunteers and coordinate assistance for your call.
  • To review and follow up on volunteer applications and member credentials.
  • To send operational alerts, dispatch status updates, and verification codes to callers and opted-in volunteers.
  • To process charitable donations and issue tax-deductible 501(c)(3) receipts.
  • To maintain system security and prevent misuse.

SMS & Mobile Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with telecommunications and infrastructure service providers (such as our SMS carrier) solely to deliver operational messages on our behalf.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with responding volunteers and essential service providers as necessary to assist you, or when required by law.

Your Choices

You may reply STOP to any operational text message at any time to opt out, or reply HELP for assistance.
