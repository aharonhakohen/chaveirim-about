What We Do

Free non-medical emergency roadside and community assistance handled by equipped volunteer responders.

Vehicle & Emergency Lockouts

Immediate priority response for children locked in vehicles or homes, plus rapid non-destructive vehicle lockout assistance.

Dead Battery & Jump Starts

Commercial-grade portable jump packs and electrical diagnostics to get stranded motorists safely back on the road.

Flat Tire Assistance

On-scene spare tire installation, tire inflation, and roadside wheel assistance day or night in all weather conditions.

Stuck Vehicle & Storm Recovery

Winch-outs from snow, mud, sand, or roadside ditches, as well as emergency generator and storm response support.

Residential Home Lockouts

Safe assistance for residents locked out of their homes, with immediate emergency dispatch when a child is inside.

Search & Rescue Operations

Coordinated volunteer ground search teams and specialized lighting and utility equipment for missing-person searches.
